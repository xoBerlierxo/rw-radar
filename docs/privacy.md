# RW Radar: Privacy note

The app shows this same text under Settings → Privacy. Keep the two in sync.

**What we collect**
- An anonymous ID that Firebase creates when you first open the app. It is not linked to your name or account.
- The companies you follow and the role families you pick (for example Engineering or Data).
- Your notification settings.
- Usage events (for example "followed a company" or "opened a notification") and crash reports, so we can fix bugs.

**What we don't collect**
- Your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location or résumé.

**Where it's stored**
- In Google Firebase (Firestore, Analytics and Crashlytics).

**How to delete it**
- Uninstalling the app (or clearing its data) removes the anonymous ID from your phone. Your follows can't be
  recovered after that.
- To delete the data stored on the server for your ID, email <OWNER_CONTACT_EMAIL> and we will remove it.

**News and job links**
- News headlines link to the publisher's own website. Job links open the company's own job posting.
  RW Radar doesn't store article text.
- The "Get referred on ReferralWorld" button opens the ReferralWorld Careers website. RW Radar doesn't
  share any data with ReferralWorld.
