# RW Radar: Install guide (closed test)

RW Radar isn't on the Play Store yet. You install it directly from an APK file. It takes about 2 minutes.

> Screenshots: add them from 2 phone brands (e.g. Samsung and Xiaomi/Pixel) before Day 13.

## 1. Download the APK
Open the link you received on your Android phone and download `rw-radar-<version>.apk`.

## 2. Allow installs from your browser or Files app
When you open the file, Android asks for permission:
- Tap **Settings** → turn on **Allow from this source** → go back.
- (Samsung: *Install unknown apps* → choose Chrome or My Files → **Allow**.)

## 3. Install
- Tap **Install**.
- If **Play Protect** warns that the app is unknown, tap **More details → Install anyway**.
  This happens because the app isn't on the Play Store yet.

## 4. Open RW Radar
1. Pick the role families you care about.
2. Follow at least 3 companies.
3. **Allow notifications** when asked. Without this you won't get job or funding alerts.

### If notifications are late
Some phones (Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Realme, OnePlus) pause apps in the background to save battery.
Go to *Settings → Apps → RW Radar → Battery* and choose **No restrictions / Don't optimise**.

## Updating
When we send a new version, download it and install it the same way. It installs **over** the old version
and **keeps your follows**.

**Warning:** If you *uninstall* the app or *clear its data*, your follows are lost. You will need to
follow your companies again. The app uses an anonymous ID with no sign-in.
